Privacy Policy

X-Twitter Quickdelete

Effective 2026-08-08  ·  Applies to the Chrome/Edge/Brave extension "X-Twitter Quickdelete"

In short: this extension does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data, browsing history, or tweet content to us or to any third party. Everything it reads on the page, it reads locally in your browser to decide which button to show you or which tweet to delete — nothing is sent off your device except the optional one-time payment, which is handled entirely by our payment processor.

What the extension does

X-Twitter Quickdelete runs only on x.com and twitter.com. It adds a delete button to tweets in your action bar, and an optional bulk delete-by-keyword panel. Both features work by automating clicks through X's own delete menu on your behalf — they do not use any private API or credentials.

Data we read, and why

  • Tweet text — read locally, only to check whether it matches a keyword you typed, for the optional bulk-delete feature. This text is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Your logged-in handle and a tweet's author handle — read locally, only to decide whether the delete button should be active for that tweet or show as disabled. Never saved or sent anywhere.

None of this is transmitted to us, sold, or shared with any third party.

What we store locally

  • One flag remembering whether you've closed the extension's floating panel, so it stays hidden until you reopen it from the toolbar icon.
  • A paid/unpaid status flag for the optional bulk-delete feature, managed by our payment library (see below). This is a true/false value, not any payment detail.

Both live only in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) and are never transmitted by us.

Payments

The optional bulk-delete unlock is a one-time $0.99 charge processed entirely by ExtensionPay and Stripe. We never see, receive, or store your card details — that flow happens on their pages, governed by their own privacy policies.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or transfer your data to third parties.
  • We don't use any data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose (deleting your own tweets).
  • We don't use data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
  • We don't run on, or request access to, any site other than x.com and twitter.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Material changes will be reflected in the extension's Chrome Web Store listing.
